# SkillBridge Academy Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea
SkillBridge Academy is a modern, prestigious learning institution. Its traditional circular academic seal pairs a wide-winged falcon with rising sunburst rays, a curved SKILLBRIDGE name, and a folded ACADEMY ribbon. The falcon represents focus and forward motion; the rising rays signify discovery and ambition.

## Color palette
- Deep Royal Indigo — `#1E1B4B` (primary identity, headings, and trust)
- Rich Teal Slate — `#0F766E` (secondary accent and academic vitality)
- Warm Copper Gold — `#D97706` (crest details, highlights, and calls to action)
- Crisp Off-White — `#F8FAFC` (background and contrast)
- Alert Amber — `#B45309`
- Alert Red — `#B91C1C`

## Typography
- Web and presentation: Inter, system sans-serif
- Academy wordmark: Georgia / Times New Roman serif
- PDF and formal assets: Helvetica
- Headings: bold, high contrast; body: regular, 1.5 line height

## Crest and logo use
- The circular seal has a double ring, gold sunburst, wide-winged falcon with feather linework, curved SKILLBRIDGE lettering, and folded ACADEMY ribbon.
- Preserve clear space equal to half the icon width.
- Use the crest lockup on crisp off-white or pale backgrounds; use the simplified crest favicon at small sizes.
- Do not stretch, recolor, add effects, or place over busy imagery.

## Ethical & Copyright Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Never promise guaranteed jobs.
- [ ] Never promise fixed monthly dollar income.
- [ ] Never promise overnight freelancing success.
- [ ] State that freelance outcomes depend on skill, portfolio quality, effort, and market opportunities.
- [ ] Display exact verified fees and schedules only.
- [ ] Identify scholarship requests for human coordinator review; never imply approval.
- [ ] Use only original or properly licensed photography, icons, fonts, and music.
- [ ] Keep session recordings and certificates described as stated in the verified client brief.
- [ ] Preserve source attribution for all market-research citations.
- [ ] Review every new campaign for factual and income claims before publishing.
